# Seer — six interfaces, one continuous line

A design study for **poidh bounty #38** — redesigning Seer's six connected interfaces around the
Electric Foundation identity.

| # | interface | demo |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Seer homepage | [index.html](docs/index.html) |
| 2 | Seer app (market explorer) | [app.html](docs/app.html) |
| 3 | Deep (many-outcome questions) | [deep.html](docs/deep.html) |
| 4 | Futarchy (conditional measures) | [futarchy.html](docs/futarchy.html) |
| 5 | Opportunity Markets (selection forecasts) | [opportunity-markets.html](docs/opportunity-markets.html) |
| 6 | Risk Pricing (cross-protocol risk) | [risk-pricing.html](docs/risk-pricing.html) |

**The idea.** Seer is one protocol wearing six interfaces. Most dashboards hide that: each app
looks like its own product, and the probability you read in Deep is not obviously the probability
you trade in the app. This system makes the continuity visible. A single accent line runs through
all six screens - it starts where a question is asked, becomes a price in the explorer, splits into
a distribution in Deep, forks into the pass/fail pair in Futarchy, narrows to a selection odds
curve in Opportunity Markets, and settles as one comparable number per protocol in Risk Pricing.

**Departures from the EF kit.** I could not open the Figma brand kit (it needs a Figma seat), so the
palette is derived from Seer's own live site instead: deep ink surfaces, one mint accent, one sky
accent, and semantic green/amber/red reserved strictly for probability and risk direction. I kept
the restraint the EF identity implies - one accent, generous whitespace, a mono face for numbers
only - and spent the novelty on the connective tissue instead: the continuous line, and the fact
that every screen is legibly part of the same system.
